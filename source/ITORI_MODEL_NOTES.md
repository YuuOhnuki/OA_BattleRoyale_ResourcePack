# Itori / Eye of Perception 1.9.29

Native Java item model: assets/oa/models/item/itori.json (299 cuboids).
Silver raised edges and curls; angular disconnected diamond-like frame; top/bottom purple gems; a closed silver great-circle band following the central violet sphere; two detached silver pearls in the gaps.
The rear face is finished to match the supplied frontal image.
The palette is saved in ITORI_PALETTE.json; texture atlas is assets/oa/textures/item/itori.png.
ITORI_PREVIEW.png is generated from the actual model JSON, not a Minecraft screenshot.

Seven short-lived FX meshes: star sigil, ascending glyph column, spherical shield grid, frost wave, layered flame blade, flame ground sigil, orange movement glyph.
FX textures are newly drawn procedural assets. No screenshots are used as in-game textures.
